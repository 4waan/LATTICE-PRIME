The record

Privacy is a quantity, not a toggle

Hide-or-show is a mixer, and a mixer is unbankable for regulated collateral. Every metered venue publication spends from a governed budget under a published ceiling. When the budget is gone, the event is withheld while the valid transaction still completes.

The EVM cannot emit “I said nothing.” A Hedera consensus topic can. An independent verifier can reconcile that silence against the transaction and the governed budget.

The lattice governs venue events. Contract storage, calldata, ATS events, and wallet activity remain public.

On Hedera

Four facts that have to live on this chain.

Fair order, a real security token, a silence record the EVM cannot emit, and a mark that is never written down.

Ordering

Consensus timestamp

There is no mempool to front-run and no gas auction to lose. The commit is sequenced before anyone can read it.

The instrument

HTS / ATS holds

The bond is a partitioned security token. The venue escrows through an ATS hold. It never takes custody.

The record

A consensus topic

Charge, ceiling, silence, and checkpoint land on HCS, then reconcile against spentBits. Contracts cannot reach the topic; a relay is the specified pattern.

The mark

Network rate 0x168

The cash leg is Hedera’s own exchange-rate precompile, because Chainlink on this network refuses a contract caller. markToMarket stores nothing.

In plain terms

What Lattice Prime actually does.

You hold a tokenised bond. You can trade it through a sealed ticket or finance against it through a reviewed agreement. Prove eligibility without publishing the underlying credential attributes, open the order only after its round closes, and receive a verifiable record of every venue disclosure against a budget it cannot exceed.
