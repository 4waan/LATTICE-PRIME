Why it exists

The two accidents

A house that could read the book, or a book nobody could sum.

Institutional trading produced the same failure twice, in opposite directions. Most venues still pick one.

The operator held the cleartext

Crossfinder, $84.3m

Credit Suisse transmitted subscriber orders out of its dark pool to other desks. Confidentiality was a contract term. The machine could read the book anyway.

Nobody held the sum

Archegos, $5.5bn

One fund stacked the same risk across several primes. Privacy worked exactly as sold. No counterparty, and no supervisor, could see the line.

This venue refuses that choice. Size leaves as a hash. Disclosure is a budget, not a mixer.

The record

Privacy is a quantity, not a toggle

Hide-or-show is a mixer, and a mixer is unbankable for regulated collateral. Every leak here spends bits from a published ceiling. When the budget is gone the event is withheld; the trade still prints.

The EVM cannot emit “I said nothing.” A Hedera consensus topic can. A supervisor can resolve the same position on a published policy without asking the holder.

On Hedera

Four facts that have to live on this chain.

Fair order, a real security token, a silence record the EVM cannot emit, and a mark that is never written down.

Ordering

Consensus timestamp

There is no mempool to front-run and no gas auction to lose. The commit is sequenced before anyone can read it.

The instrument

HTS / ATS holds

The bond is a partitioned security token. The venue escrows through an ATS hold. It never takes custody.

The record

A consensus topic

Charge, refusal, silence, and checkpoint land on HCS, then reconcile against spentBits. Contracts cannot reach the topic; a relay is the specified pattern.

The mark

Network rate 0x168

The cash leg is Hedera’s own exchange-rate precompile, because Chainlink on this network refuses a contract caller. markToMarket stores nothing.

In plain terms

What Lattice Prime actually does.

A repo is borrowing cash against collateral, with an agreement to buy the collateral back. You hold a tokenised bond and you want to sell a lot of it without the market seeing you coming. Lattice Prime lets you prove you are allowed to trade without handing over your identity, put the order on chain as a sealed hash, and open it only once the round has closed. Whatever the venue does disclose about you comes out of a budget it cannot exceed.
