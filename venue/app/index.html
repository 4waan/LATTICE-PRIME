What makes it different

Six things a normal order book cannot do.

Each one is enforced by a contract, not by a policy document. Point at a card to read it.Swipe through them.

01

Orders are sealed, not merely hidden

A commitment is a keccak of your order. Side, price and size are never on the wire until you choose to open them.

02

Eligibility without identity

A zero-knowledge proof of tier and jurisdiction, checked against an issuer root that rolls at every KYC epoch boundary.

ZkKycRegistry · PLONK
03

A disclosure budget the chain enforces

Every read that leaks something spends bits from a per-epoch budget. When the budget is gone the venue stops answering.

SeamJournal.spentBits
04

Cancel and reveal never overlap

Two disjoint windows off one timestamp. There is no instant where you can watch a reveal land and still pull your own order.

cancellableUntil(id)
05

Margin you can believe, or told that you cannot

The watcher returns the position and, separately, whether the log behind it was audible. A row can be permitted and unreliable at once.

MarginWatch.watch
06

Fair ordering, and a fee you can predict

Hedera orders transactions by consensus timestamp. There is no mempool to front-run you in and no gas auction to lose.

What powers it

Four pieces of industry infrastructure, used as intended.

None of this is reimplemented. The venue is the part that did not exist yet.

Settlement Hedera

Consensus timestamps give fair ordering with no mempool, and the EVM gives the venue somewhere to live. Fees are quoted, not auctioned.

The instrument Asset Tokenization Studio

The bond is a real ATS security token: partitions, holds and an external compliance hook. The venue escrows through the hold, never by taking custody.

The proof Circom and snarkjs

The eligibility circuit compiles to PLONK over a universal ceremony, so this project ran no trusted setup of its own. Proving happens on the holder’s machine; the private inputs never leave it.

In plain terms

What SeamMe actually does.

You hold a tokenised bond and you want to sell a lot of it without the market seeing you coming. SeamMe lets you prove you are allowed to trade without handing over your identity, put the order on chain as a sealed hash, and open it only once the round has closed. Whatever the venue does disclose about you comes out of a budget it cannot exceed.
