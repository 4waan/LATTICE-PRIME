The price the collateral is marked at

PrimeOracle · two legs

Two legs, and only one of them is the venue's. The seated panel publishes LPRC's clean price in USD, because a private bond has no other quote. HBAR/USD is somebody else's number, because the bond is denominated in USD and this venue settles in tinybars, and nothing on chain converted between them before this. The mark is the first divided by the second, and RepoVault.markToMarket multiplies it by a repo's own lot in memory and stores nothing.

The cash seat is a governed address in Chainlink's AggregatorV3Interface shape, and on this chain it does not hold a Chainlink feed. Chainlink runs HBAR/USD on Hedera and its proxies refuse a contract caller with No access while answering decimals() to anyone, on testnet and mainnet alike. So the seat holds an adapter over Hedera's own exchange rate at 0x168, which is the rate every transaction fee on this network is priced at. The measurement, including the on-chain probe that shows what an eth_call cannot, is probes/chainlink-hedera.out.
