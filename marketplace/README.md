# Marketplace study

Evidence for two questions. What do real trading venues actually hide, and what
does Hedera already publish about the securities on it today.

Both were answered by measurement rather than by reading marketing material.
The probes in `probes/` are reproducible and need no account or key.

    venues.md          six venues, compared on what they conceal
    hedera-exposure.md what a tokenised fund on Hedera reveals to anyone
    findings.md        what the study changed in the design

## The admission rule

A fact entered this study only if it answered four questions: which piece of
information it concerns, at what resolution and to whom and for how long it is
revealed, by what mechanism, and who has to be honest for that to hold.

Anything that could not answer all four was marketing, and was not carried
forward. This is a deliberately harsh filter and it rejected most of what is
written about market privacy.
