# Demo script

What the demonstration shows, in order, and why each beat is there.

## The shape

Five minutes, four beats.

**The problem, shown not described.** A live query against the public network
returns the complete holder register of a real regulated fund, with per-account
compliance status, requiring no credential of any kind. This is run rather than
screenshotted, because the point is that anyone can do it.

**Registration.** A participant joins the venue by proving eligibility rather
than being added to a published list. The same query is run again and returns
nothing about them.

**A repo, opened and margined.** Collateral moves, cash moves, both legs settle
or neither does. The market sees a commitment. The counterparty sees what the
trade requires. A margin call fires and discloses a boolean rather than a price.

**The supervisor view.** The regulator resolves the same position to exact
figures, on a published policy, without the participant's cooperation and
without asking anyone's permission.

## Why this order

The exposure has to come first because everything after it is unmotivated
otherwise. An audience that has not seen the register is being asked to care
about a solution to a problem they do not believe exists.

The supervisor beat comes last because it is the one that separates this from a
mixer, and it is the objection an institutional audience will already be forming
during the privacy beats.

## What is cut

Circuit internals, the proving system choice, gas figures, and the seam
analysis. All of it is in the repository. None of it survives contact with a
five minute slot, and an audience that wants it will read.
